# Google Play Listing

## App name

Momentary Momentos

## Short description

Capture 10-second video memories and rediscover the moments that matter.

## Full description

Life is made of small moments. Momentary Momentos helps you capture them before
they pass and rediscover them when you need a reason to smile.

Record a quick 10-second video, add an emotion or custom tag, and save a little
piece of your day. Over time, your collection becomes a personal library of
people, places, milestones, and everyday memories worth revisiting.

CAPTURE THE MOMENT

Save meaningful moments without interrupting them. The focused 10-second format
makes recording quick, simple, and intentional.

ORGANIZE IT YOUR WAY

Add emotions and custom tags to make each memory personal and easier to find.

REDISCOVER YOUR MEMORIES

Let past moments resurface and bring forgotten smiles, celebrations, and
everyday experiences back into view.

KEEP YOUR MEMORIES WITH YOU

Browse and play your saved momentos whenever you want. You can also request a
free email export of your memories.

STAY IN CONTROL

Your account includes clear privacy controls. You can request an export or
permanently delete your account and associated data from within the app.

Momentary Momentos is a simple video diary for capturing the little experiences
that make life memorable - one 10-second momento at a time.
